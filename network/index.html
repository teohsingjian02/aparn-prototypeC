Person

Roopesh Sitharan

Director of APARN (from 2026) · Network convenor
Faculty of Creative Multimedia, Multimedia University (MMU), Cyberjaya

A multidisciplinary practitioner, educator, curator, researcher and artist. His projects have appeared at the Gwangju Biennale, ISEA and SIGGRAPH, and his writing in Leonardo Electronic Almanac, CTRL+P and Documenta 12.

Person

Kurniawan Adi Saputro

Network convenor
Indonesian Institute of the Arts Yogyakarta (ISI Yogyakarta)

Lecturer in the Department of Photography at ISI Yogyakarta, teaching research methods in arts and visual communication. His recent research looks at photographs in climate education, community-based festivals and climate justice. In late 2024 he built Wana Nagara, a studio around a tiny urban forest for researching culture in a time of climate crisis.

Victorian College of the Arts, University of Melbourne

Coordinator of Research for Design and Production at the Victorian College of the Arts, University of Melbourne. He wrote Artistic Research in the Future Academy (Intellect / University of Chicago Press, 2017) and sits on the Editorial Board of the Journal for Artistic Research.

Gunalan Nadarajan

Network convenor
Penny W. Stamps School of Art and Design, University of Michigan

An art theorist and curator working where art, science and technology meet. His publications have been translated into 16 languages. His curatorial work includes Documenta XI (Kassel, 2002) and ISEA2008 in Singapore.

Activity

APARN 2019

Held October 2019 · Yogyakarta, Indonesia

Where it began. Artists and researchers met in Yogyakarta, alongside the International Conference on Asia Pacific Arts Studies, to start a regional network for artistic research.

Connected to

Open APARN 2019

Activity

APARN2020

Held 3–6 November 2020 · Online

Four days of conversations about borders, oceans and solidarity across the region, after Édouard Glissant’s line “The entire world is becoming an archipelago.” Speakers included Raqs Media Collective, Léuli Eshrāghi, Yuki Kihara, Natalie King, Nuraini Juliastuti, Sutanto Mendut, Helly Minarti and Mark Teh.

Connected to

Open APARN2020

Activity

APARN2021

Held 26 November 2021 · Online

The third meeting turned the floor over to graduate researchers. PhD, Masters, Honours and independent researchers each had five minutes to introduce their project, so everyone could see the range of artistic research in the region and find future collaborators.

Connected to

Open APARN2021

Activity

#APARN2022

Held 25 November 2022 · Online

A day of research on local ecologies, extraction and our responsibilities to the planet, starting from Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak’s “Imperative to Reimagine the Planet”. Presenters came from Thailand, Aotearoa New Zealand, Australia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Austria and beyond.

Connected to

Open #APARN2022

Activity

APARN2023

Held 7 July 2023 · Yogyakarta, Indonesia

Art results from matter, and results in matter. Artists shared the materials they work with, why that matters to them, and how close attention to material can open our senses to a changing planet.

Connected to

Open APARN2023

Activity

APARN2024

Held 12–13 June 2024 · Cyberjaya, Malaysia

How do we make order and meaning from the world? Two days in Cyberjaya explored art as a way of reasoning: how artworks, as philosophical simulations, produce new knowledge, and how art and technology together can imagine liveable futures.

Connected to

Open APARN2024

Activity

APARN2025

Held 1–3 July 2025 · Bangkok, Thailand

The largest APARN gathering so far, at the Sodsai Pantoomkomol Centre for Dramatic Arts. Researchers, artists and community organisers asked how artistic research can enrich community life, and how communities create new ways of knowing and making.
