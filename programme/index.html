Take part

Meet in the region each year, share your progress during the SAR Conference, and add your work to a year-end collection. One contribution can carry you through all three.

Three ways in

  • APARN Conference

    Every year, a different host in the region opens its doors to artists, researchers and communities.

    Next: APARN2027, RMIT University Vietnam Details to be confirmed

  • APARN Gathering at the SAR Conference

    Each year, members share short research updates of 5–10 minutes during the SAR Conference.

    Next: SAR 2027, Bratislava Details to be confirmed

  • APARN Annual Publication

    A year-end collection of members’ research in progress, using the same contribution.
